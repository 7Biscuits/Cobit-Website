About us:
CoBit is a technology-focused organisation empowering students to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world skills. Through hands-on workshops, bootcamps, robotics, coding, competitions, and practical projects, CoBit encourages innovation, creativity, and problem-solving. With experienced mentors and a learn-by-doing approach, we help students explore emerging technologies, build meaningful projects, and prepare for future opportunities in the digital world.

FAQs

What is CoBit?
CoBit is a technology-focused organisation that helps students develop practical skills through hands-on workshops, bootcamps, competitions, hackathons, and real-world projects.
What does CoBit do?
CoBit creates learning experiences where students explore technology, build projects, solve problems, and develop skills beyond traditional classroom learning.
Who can join CoBit programmes?
Our programmes are designed for school students, college students, beginners, and aspiring innovators. Eligibility may vary depending on the specific event or programme.
Do I need prior technical knowledge to participate?
Not necessarily. Many of our programmes are beginner-friendly and introduce concepts from the ground up. Check the requirements of the specific programme before registering.
What kind of workshops and events does CoBit organise?
CoBit organises technology workshops, robotics activities, coding bootcamps, AI/ML learning programmes, game development sessions, competitions, and hackathons. Offerings vary by event.
Will I build a real project during a workshop?
Our programmes emphasise practical learning and project-building wherever the format allows. The exact project and deliverables depend on the event.
Are the sessions theoretical or hands-on?
We focus on learning by doing, combining explanations and demonstrations with guided activities, experimentation, and practical implementation.
Who conducts the workshops?
Workshops are conducted by programme mentors and team members with relevant experience. Mentor details are shared where applicable for each event.
How can I register for an event?
Visit the relevant event page, review the details and eligibility criteria, and complete the registration form. Follow the instructions provided after submitting your registration.
Is there a registration fee?
Fees vary by programme. Check the relevant event page for the registration fee, inclusions, and any applicable offers.
Will I receive a certificate?
Certificate availability and eligibility requirements depend on the programme. Please refer to the specific event page for the applicable details.
What if I have a question after registering?
Contact the CoBit team through the contact details provided on the website or in your registration confirmation. Include your event name and registration details so we can assist you.
Can my college or school collaborate with CoBit?
Yes, schools, colleges, student communities, and institutions can reach out to explore workshops, events, technology programmes, and other potential collaborations.
Can organisations become sponsors or partners?
Yes. Organisations interested in sponsorships, partnerships, or supporting student-focused technology initiatives can contact our team to discuss suitable opportunities.
How can I join the CoBit team or volunteer?
Keep an eye on our official channels for openings and opportunities, or contact us directly to express your interest in contributing to upcoming initiatives.
How do I contact CoBit?
Use the contact information or enquiry form available on our website. For event-specific questions, mention the programme you're interested in so we can direct your enquiry appropriately.
