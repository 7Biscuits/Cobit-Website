About Us

Empowering students with firsthand career experience.

Most students are expected to pick a lifetime career before they have ever tried one. We bring a working version of tech, finance, marketing, management, and startup life directly into schools, so life choices are made with authentic confidence instead of guesswork.

Vision

Clarity Before Choice

A future where every young person embarks on higher education and vocational journeys grounded in authentic, firsthand workplace understanding.

Mission

Active Simulation in Schools

To bring practical, mentor-led industry simulations into high school classrooms — connecting students with active professionals, real toolsets, and startup internships.

Purpose

Democratizing Opportunity

To replace passive career lectures with active doing, breaking down informational and socioeconomic barriers to high-impact modern industries.

Real experience

Students do the actual work, not just hear about it.

In your school

We come to the classroom, so every student can take part.

A few hours at a time

Short, focused sessions that fit smoothly around the school day.
